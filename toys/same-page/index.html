How to play

Each player is dealt one name, shown to their partner but not to them. Ask yes/no questions to work out your own.

  • Ask your partner yes/no questions about your name. They answer from memory; they can't say or describe the name you're wearing.
  • Don't look anything up during play. If you don't know an answer, say "I don't know"; it doesn't count as a question.
  • The names come from one shared Wikipedia article; both are linked from its first paragraph.
  • Article context fills in over time; use it to sharpen your questions.
  • The first player to say their own name out loud wins. The app doesn't referee the race.
