Two players, one Wikipedia article, two secret names.

Dealing the cards…

How to play

Every game picks one Wikipedia article and two names linked from its first paragraph. Each player is shown only their partner's name.

  • Ask your partner yes/no questions to work out what your own name is.
  • Answer truthfully, from memory. Never say or describe the name you can see — only answer questions.
  • Don't look anything up or search during play. If you don't know, say "I don't know"; it doesn't count as a question.
  • Both names are links from the first paragraph of the shared word's article.
  • Figure out your own name to win. The app doesn't referee the race.
